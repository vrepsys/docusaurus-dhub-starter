This site comes with an optional blog, and this is its first post. Blog posts are regular Markdown files in the `blog` folder, and you can write and edit them in [Dhub](https://dhub.dev) exactly like docs pages.

{/* truncate */}

A few things worth knowing:

- The part of a post above the *truncate* marker becomes the preview on the blog index page. In Dhub, you'll see the marker as a block you can move.
- Posts get their date from the filename (this one is `2026-07-20-welcome.md`) and their URL from the `slug` in the frontmatter.
- Authors and tags are defined once in `blog/authors.yml` and `blog/tags.yml`, then referenced from each post's frontmatter.

Don't need a blog? Removing it is covered in [Make it yours](/docs/make-it-yours).
